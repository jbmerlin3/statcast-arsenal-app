## 1. What the app is

A Shiny app that scouts one pitcher's arsenal from Statcast. It is
**employer-facing** — the link gets sent to clubs — so a broken or stale page
argues against Jonathan personally. That framing drives most of the design
decisions in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Live:** `jonathanmerlin.shinyapps.io/pitcher-arsenal/`

**Five tabs:** Movement, Usage, Characteristics, Heat Maps, Search.

**How data flows.** A nightly GitHub Actions chain pulls Savant, folds it into a
season store held as a **release asset** (not in git, 108 MB), rebuilds
`data/app_data.rds` and `data/league_ref.rds`, and redeploys. The app bundles its
own data, so **the live page only moves when something redeploys it.**

**Two things everything else hangs off:**

- `app_data.rds` — one row per pitch, ~636k rows, 33 columns. The column contract
  is `APP_DATA_COLS` in `R/features.R`.
- `league_ref.rds` — precomputed league percentiles at 1-point increments, keyed
  by pitch_type × p_throws × stand × count_bucket × metric. **Nothing scans the
  league inside a reactive.** It is read in ~44 places across 12 files.

**Architecture.** Everything in `R/` is a pure function over a frame, so it runs
from the console with no server. `app.R` is thin wiring. Adding a metric usually
means editing *data* in `R/theme.R` (`METRIC_SPEC`, `PITCH_SHAPE_DIRECTION`),
not logic elsewhere.

## 4. Decisions Jonathan made. Do not silently revisit.

Each was argued and measured. If you think one is wrong, **say so and ask.**

1. **Every window is shaded, including a single game.** `below_floor` renders
   filled, normal black text, keeping only its parenthetical n. His words: *"i
   know this is statistically a small sample, but this is what i want... it shows
   on any given day how he did."* A 60% whiff on 5 swings renders deep red with
   `(5)`. **That `(n)` is the only thing qualifying the number. It is pinned by
   test. Never drop it.**
2. **Release traits grade on uniqueness** (`extreme`, `2*|p-50|`). **Extension is
   NOT extreme** — short extension is not a weapon.
3. **`rel_side` negated but NOT arm-side mirrored.** Righty positive, lefty
   negative. It was mirrored briefly and reverted the same day.
4. **VAA is raw, not residualised on `plate_z`.** The residual already exists in
   `02_StuffPlus/sc_fit_vaa_adj()` and is priced into Stuff+ two cells away.
5. **RV only, no RV/100.** RV/100 was built and removed the same day: split-half
   r = 0.02–0.10 at every sample. RV is a counting stat and takes no percentile.
6. **No footnotes on the traits table.** Three things are consequently invisible
   and unrecoverable from it: whether Stuff+ is stale, that a sweeper's Stuff+ is
   really a slider's, and whether a percentile came from a coarser cut.
7. **Release *side* deliberately NOT in Search.** A slider spanning −3.4 to +3.0
   is meaningless until you pick a hand. Open design question, not an oversight.

## 5. Recent troubles, and what they teach

### The deploy gate broke twice in one day

`deploy.R` decides whether to redeploy by comparing `app_data` date, `game_logs`
date, code SHA, and now a `content` digest.

- **A backfill changes values but no date and no commit.** A 200-day re-pull
  refreshed 40,000+ arm angles into the store, the gate compared identical dates
  and an identical SHA, and **skipped the deploy while reporting success.** That
  is why `content` exists.
- **Then I broke it worse.** I added `content` to `data_dates()` and made
  `deployed_dates()` require it, but the stamp **write** still listed four fields
  by hand. Every stamp was unusable, so the gate matched nothing and **every run
  redeployed unconditionally** — 4/day instead of ~1, burning shinyapps hours.
  Fixed in PR #5 by building the stamp from `have` plus a round-trip assertion.

**The lesson worth carrying:** a `NULL` from `deployed_dates()` is
indistinguishable from "no stamp yet", which legitimately deploys. **Gate bugs
are silent in both directions.** Always read the stamp after a deploy.

### Arm angle was missing for eleven dates, unnoticed for three weeks

Not availability, not a rename — verified by pulling through the chain's own
`pull_season_statcast()`, which returned `arm_angle` numeric at 0.41% NA.

**It was the re-pull window.** `repull_days` was 7. Arm angle's backfill lands
later than that, and a date that falls out of the window is never re-fetched, so
it freezes at NA forever. August sat at 46.8% NA while Savant held the values.

Fixed: window raised to 21, and `verify_traits.R` **tier D** now fails if any
date older than 3 days exceeds 25% NA.

**I was wrong about this three times** before getting it right — first "Savant
stopped publishing it", then "one-day lag". Jonathan pushed back correctly each
time. Do not assume a data gap is upstream until you have queried upstream.

### CI failure emails, last two days

Two runs failed, both **shinyapps.io server-side during deploy** (`Unable to
acquire lock`, then `GET(): Internal Server Error`). The pull and rebuild
succeeded both times; a failed deploy just leaves the previous version serving.

They started appearing because **my gate bug quadrupled deploy volume**, which
quadrupled exposure. Now that the gate works, they should stop.

**If they keep arriving at the same rate, it is not volume and needs a real
look.** That is the thing to watch over the next day.

### Open design questions, in the order I would raise them

4. **The sweeper Stuff+ caveat.** Gone with the traits footnotes. FanGraphs
   grades ST and SV under SL, so a sweeper's Stuff+ is a slider's and the table
   now shows it unmarked. `fg_exact` still carries the fact. Best fix is probably
   a marker on the number rather than a returning footnote.
5. **Release side in Search.** Needs a decision on what a two-hand slider means.
   Mirroring by the selected `p_throws` is the obvious answer.
6. **Sample visibility in the results table.** STRIKE%, CSW% and IN-ZONE% show
   their sample nowhere in that table, because their denominator is pitch count
   and COUNT moved to Traits. **This is the only thing on the page that depends
   on the two tables staying adjacent and identically ordered.**

### Bigger, measured but not built

7. **A window-matched league reference.** The statistically correct fix for
   ranking a one-game rate: compare a start against other *starts*, not against
   seasons. Rejected on cost, not on merit — `league_ref` is read in 44 places
   and would go from one table to one per window size. Revisit if the shading
   ever feels overstated.
8. **A pitcher-level card.** Jonathan's original reference image was a
   pitcher-level percentile-bar layout, not a per-pitch-type table. We kept the
   table grain deliberately (release slot varies by pitch type and that is a real
   tell). A card *above* the tables was floated and never built.
9. **Columns considered and rejected, with measurements** — do not re-propose
   cold: `zWhiff%` (r=0.55 at n=25, needs ~4× the sample of whiff%), `HH%` per
   pitch type (r=0.44 at n=25, median 17 BBE per cell — it belongs at pitcher
   level, where it already is), `RV/100`. `SWING%` and `PUT-AWAY%` were offered
   as the two that hold up and were declined.

## 7. Verification, and what each check can and cannot see

```bash
for t in tests/*.R; do [ "$(basename $t)" = "phase1_artifacts.R" ] && continue
  echo "$(basename $t .R): $(Rscript $t 2>&1 | grep -E 'PASS|FAIL' | tail -1)"; done
Rscript scripts/phase1_check.R           # regression vs the original script
Rscript scripts/verify_traits.R          # 4 tiers: recompute, aggregate, physics, freshness
Rscript scripts/verify_arsenal_savant.R  # external, per pitcher per pitch type
```

All ten pass as of `42636a1`.

**The distinction that matters most:**

- `phase1_check` and `step3_*` compare the app to **itself or an older itself**.
  They cannot catch a number wrong since the day it landed.
- `verify_traits.R` tiers A/B recompute — **a stale NA reconciles with itself
  perfectly.** Tier D exists for exactly that.
- `verify_arsenal_savant.R` is the only external check at pitch-type grain
  (whiff% r=0.995, run value r=0.971 over 1,141 cells). But it compares against
  pitchers Savant **returns**, which excludes ones whose value is missing.
  **A source that drops its own nulls cannot be used to find nulls.**

**VAA, release height and release side have NO external ground truth anywhere.**
Verified only by internal recompute, physics, and league-value sanity.

**Recapturing a byte baseline:** `Rscript tests/step3_null_identical.R --write`.
**Diff by column first and record what moved in the file's history block** — every
prior recapture did. Compare column sets in **both** directions; my diff script
reported added columns but not dropped ones and read "nothing changed" on a table
that had just lost three.

## 8. Traps that cost real time

**gt.** `cols_width()` evaluates its formula lazily — a local variable fails at
*render* time, so bake the number into the formula string. `cols_label()` and
`cells_body()` error on absent columns. An inline lambda in a gt pipe fed
`cols_label` a non-gt object and failed several calls later naming the wrong
function; use a plain `if`.

**dplyr.** A column named `.d` partial-matches `mutate()`'s own `.data` argument
and is swallowed as a parameter — fails with `object 'x' not found` on a frame
that plainly has it. `.t` is fine; it matches no argument name.

**Savant.** The custom leaderboard **echoes any selection name back as a header
and fills it with NA** when the field is not real. A bad field name looks exactly
like a successful fetch. Check for all-NA before trusting it. Also: `plate_z`
goes negative (bounced pitches extrapolate below zero), eephus pitches reach
−34° VAA, and Tyler Rogers releases at 1.26 ft. Bound checks by what arms
actually do, not what a typical arm does.

**Test fixtures go stale in a specific way.** `tests/fixtures/` is tracked and
deliberately frozen. A fixture that hardcodes a pitcher expected to be in a
particular *state* will break as the reference grows — the fallback fixture went
stale three times before being made self-locating. If you need a new column on a
frozen fixture, derive it from the fixture itself or join 1:1, prove the join,
and assert every prior column is byte-identical.

**Never download the release store over `~/baseball-store/`.** An interrupted
download truncates it to an unreadable file. I did this. Download to a temp path
and move it into place.
