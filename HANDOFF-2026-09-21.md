## 3. What is on `context-tab` (uncommitted)

### The Context tab, as it now reads

Answers three questions for one pitcher: is he funky, what makes him good, how
do we attack him.

- **Top control:** Pitch type only. Pitcher, dates and batter side are global.
- **Profile (left):** stat row (listed height, arm slot as a scouting label,
  release height, release vs what slot and height predict, release-point
  rarity), then a Savant-style percentile chart for the selected pitch type.
- **Attack plan (right):** Sit on (mix when behind), Two strikes, Hunt (most
  and least hittable pitch by xwOBA), Approach (zone%, chase%, platoon).
- **Arsenal (full width):** every pitch type, usage bar, whiff% and xwOBA as
  percentile bars.
- **Details (collapsed):** peer-group controls, release scatter, nested
  baselines table, comparables.

The **Outliers** tab existed briefly and was removed by request. Its
`outliers_gt()` is deleted.

## 4. Decisions Jonathan made. Do not silently revisit.

1. **HB displays raw, ranks mirrored.** Every `league_ref` lookup for `hb` must
   multiply by `arm_side_sign(p_throws)`. Done in `resolve_table()` and
   `league_percentiles()`.
2. **Fallback cell: dagger yes, bold no.** Bold made the least certain cell the
   loudest. Measured: fallback is 0.59% of cells, 0% on All Batters.
3. **No league crosses on the Movement chart.**
4. **Extension is NOT in the release-height model.** Height and slot are who
   he is, extension is what he does. Residual correlates −0.44 with extension
   and that is disclosed, pinned by test.
5. **Low-support arm slots are flagged, never capped.** Fewer than 10 same-hand
   pitchers within ±5°. Catches both tails (Rogers −60.7°, Vesia 69.9°).
6. **Fixed-K = 25 is the default cohort.** Fixed windows gave 22 vs 61 and half
   the spread between baselines was window width.
7. **"Both matched" baseline dropped.** N too small to carry a number.
8. **Percentile bubbles are red above league, blue below, on every trait**, and
   only the high-side word is printed.
9. **Arsenal bars fill to the PITCHER percentile** (100 minus it for xwOBA), so
   longer and redder always mean better for him. The raw version drew a long bar
   for a bad xwOBA.
10. **No barrel% per pitch.** Median cell is 17 BBE, about 1.3 barrels; xwOBA
    already encodes speed and angle; would need a data contract change.
11. **No location chart on Context.** Heat Maps already draws it.
12. **No plotly.** Hover uses `shiny::nearPoints()`.

## 5. Measured, so it is not re-litigated

- **Percentile audit:** 25,614 tab percentiles vs an independent recompute.
  Median deviation 0.75 points (the 1-point storage grid), r ≥ 0.9994 on every
  metric. Worst: RHP curveball xwOBA, a 39-pitcher cell packed into .330–.345,
  up to 11 points. Fallback fires on under 1.1% of lookups on any batter side.
- **Listed height barely matters:** R² 0.667 on arm angle alone, 0.740 with
  height. The "tall man, low slot" story is a small effect.
- **Headrick (the reference example):** residual +3.3 in (+1.1 SD). IVB deltas
  fixed-K: league +1.91, release-height +1.60, arm-angle +2.08 (z +0.94 vs
  +1.37). The two matched cohorts **share 16 of 25 pitchers**, so they are not
  independent groups.
- **Traded pitchers were duplicated** (148 combos, 63 pitchers) until
  `pitch_team` left the grouping. LHP FF league baseline went 172 → 162.
- **A −3.6° VAA is flatter than 99% of RHP four-seams** and a 5.0 ft release
  lower than 95%; a 98 mph / 18 IVB / 5.0 ft comp query returns zero pitchers.
  These reproduce the Brody Hopkins writeup that motivated the tab.

## 7. Traps that cost time this period

- **`[[` on a named ATOMIC vector with a missing name throws "subscript out of
  bounds"**, not NULL. It killed the explorer render. Use a list.
- **`quantile()` returns a named value**; `all.equal(named TRUE, TRUE)` fails
  and prints "got TRUE wanted TRUE". Wrap in `unname()`.
- **`game_date` is character**; `which.max()` on it returns `integer(0)`. Order
  instead; zero-padded ISO sorts correctly as strings.
- **`gt::tab_header(subtitle=)` needs a `title`**, and fails at render time.
- **`nearPoints()` matches data coordinates**, so jittered points answer hovers
  with the wrong peer. `strip_data()` uses a deterministic offset.
- **Every new `league_ref` consumer for `hb` must mirror.** `league_percentiles`
  shipped without it and reported a lefty at the 0th percentile instead of the
  98th. Pinned in `step9_context.R`.
- **A fixture missing a column the aggregator reads** fails inside
  `summarise()` several frames away. `step7_search`'s `arm()` needed `vaa`.
- **Checking a render:** gt tables and Search sliders render server-side, so
  grepping served HTML proves nothing. Use the browser pane, and confirm the
  server with `lsof -ti:7717` plus an HTTP 200 first.
